About me

I am Utsab Sarker, currently living in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My father is Uttam Kumar Sarkar, and my mother is Shima Rani Pramanik. Although I now live in Dhaka, a significant part of my educational journey was spent in Rajshahi, where I completed my studies up to Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC). I also have a strong passion for writing and reading stories.

My ultimate goal is to become a skilled web developer. With that goal in mind, I am continuously learning, improving my skills, and working toward building a successful career in the tech industry.
